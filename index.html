Cooked the long way

We toast and grind our spice blends every morning. Biryani goes into a sealed pot over low heat, the way it is done in Hyderabad, so the rice steams in the juices of the meat.

Every dish is labeled for diet and heat, so first-timers and regulars both know what they are ordering.
